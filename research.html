Research & references

Behind the
campaign.

The research helped me decide who this campaign is for, why Austin fits, and how the four sides could connect to a real boutique experience.

Aritzia & market position

Elevated, every day.

Aritzia calls its approach “Everyday Luxury.” Its clothing spans workwear, essentials, activewear, and going-out looks, which gave me a reason to connect the four sides through one wardrobe.

Where it sits

Aritzia’s premium positioning sits above fast fashion and below traditional designer luxury. I looked at Reformation, Madewell, Anthropologie, Abercrombie & Fitch, and Free People as part of its competitive space.

My decision: Focus on versatile styling and an elevated boutique experience instead of price or trend-heavy messaging.

Austin & audience

A local story with a real destination.

What I found

Austin’s student, professional, creative, and social settings fit Campus, Career, Off-Duty, and Social. Aritzia already has a boutique at The Domain.

Who I chose to reach

My proposed campaign audience is Austin-area women ages 21–29, especially upper-level or graduate students and early-career professionals who want clothing that moves through different parts of the day.

This is my campaign audience, not a verified Aritzia-wide customer age range.

What changed: I started with an idea about Aritzia entering Austin. Once I found the existing boutique, I changed it to a six-week local campaign designed to build awareness, engagement, and interest in visiting The Domain.

Trends into decisions

Research I could actually use.

Versatile dressing

Aritzia already organizes looks around occasions.

My choice: Restyle selected pieces across the four sides.

Attention to value

Fashion research points to closer attention to value.

My choice: Show repeat wear instead of leading with discounts.

Social discovery

Younger consumers use social channels to discover fashion.

My choice: Use Instagram for the visual story and TikTok for outfit transitions.

Digital to boutique

Aritzia invests in digital shopping and physical boutiques.

My choice: Connect social content to the Austin Edit and The Domain styling experience.

Campaigns I looked at

What inspired the choices.

Aritzia Pre-Spring 2026

Transitional styling showed how pieces could work across different moments.

I used it: Repeat selected pieces instead of making four separate wardrobes.

Aritzia + The Juilliard School

Real activities and settings made its audience feel believable.

I used it: Place the four sides in everyday Austin settings.

Madewell: It’s the Way You Wear Them

Personal styling connected digital content with an in-store experience.

I used it: Plan a smaller styling activation at The Domain.
